Interpretation

Review priority describes the age or absence of recorded expiration information. It is not a recommendation to use, discard, or administer a product. The overdue filter applies to expired records; undated records remain visible when viewing all issues.

Only active, open vials with positive remaining contents are included. Finished, discarded, closed, and empty vials are excluded.

Undated and already-expired inventory is excluded from the FEFO allocation simulation. An expiration date is not a substitute for determining whether a product is safe or appropriate to use.

This page does not change inventory quantities, expiration dates, vial status, protocols, or dose logs.
