Understanding FEFO reliability

Forecast results depend on both inventory records and scheduled protocol demand. Missing information can make a forecast incomplete or provisional.

Expired inventory

Inventory whose effective expiration date has already passed should not be treated as available for future FEFO allocation.

Review expired records →

Missing expiration dates

Without an expiration or beyond-use date, the forecast cannot reliably determine when inventory should leave the available pool.

Review undated records →

Incomplete protocol information

Missing protocol start dates or invalid scheduled quantities can prevent demand from being projected accurately.

How to interpret provisional shortages

A provisional shortage indicates that the available information suggests a possible inventory shortfall, but the result is not fully reliable.

Before making purchasing decisions, review the affected vial records and confirm that protocol dates and quantities are complete.

These indicators describe record completeness and forecast reliability. They are not recommendations to use, discard, purchase, or administer any compound.
