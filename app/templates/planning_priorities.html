Forecast reliability: A complete scheduled-demand estimate means the FEFO engine did not identify missing or invalid schedule data for that compound. It does not guarantee that future inventory or protocol activity will match the projection.

An incomplete schedule makes shortage and coverage estimates provisional. The displayed shortage can understate actual future demand. Already-expired stock is excluded from FEFO allocation and is not included in projected future expiration losses.

*Coverage is a demand-rate indicator derived from allocated plus remaining dated stock divided by average daily scheduled demand. It is not a date-accurate supply-exhaustion prediction. Use the FEFO first-shortage date for chronological shortage projections.

Undated inventory is excluded from FEFO allocation. Expiration losses and shortages are planning projections, not actual transactions. No changes are made to inventory or protocols.
