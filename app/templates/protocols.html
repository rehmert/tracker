Protocols

Protocols can be active schedules or hypothetical what-if plans. Saving a protocol does not record an administration or consume inventory.

Next planned shows the nearest non-hidden calendar projection within 60 days, not a confirmed or recorded administration. Inventory checks use recorded quantities, unit conversions and recorded expiration/beyond-use dates as of the planned day. They do not confirm sterility, clinical suitability, or future stock. Choose vial opens the read-only workspace; Review suggested vial preselects one candidate for manual confirmation. Neither action records anything.
