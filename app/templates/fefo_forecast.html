Forecast assumptions

Only stock with a recorded expiration or beyond-use date is included in allocation. Undated stock is displayed separately. Inventory dated on a scheduled event's day is conservatively excluded before that event.

Projected expiration loss represents unused inventory remaining when its recorded date is reached. This is a planning scenario, not a prediction of actual waste. No purchases, inventory changes, or dosing changes are simulated.
