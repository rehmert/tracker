How are these priorities calculated?

Groups are ordered by affected vial count, then expired vial count, compound name, and unit. This is a record-review order, not a medical urgency or safety ranking.

Quantities are grouped by compound and unit. Values in mg and IU are not combined.

The summary includes all affected records, regardless of the filters applied to the individual-vial table below.
