Waterfall reconstitution · 2–6 vials

Record one measured initial BAC volume carried through multiple dry, unused vials of the same compound. The resulting solution is represented by one blend record and the original vial inventory is consumed. Nominal math assumes complete transfers and ignores powder displacement and handling loss. This does not establish sterility or compatibility.

Source order

First selected vial receives the initial BAC volume; each subsequent vial receives the preceding solution. The final physical container is the last selected vial.
