Reconstitution / 3 mL cartridge what-if

Each dry source vial is reconstituted first, before a measured portion is withdrawn for blending. Enter your target dose per peptide and desired blend draw volume. In automatic mode, the planner calculates source-vial BAC volumes using an explicit equal-withdrawal allocation; manual mode uses your entered BAC values. The planner calculates the final volume required by all peptide targets and any additional BAC water needed in the destination. A different water volume cannot fix incompatible peptide ratios.
