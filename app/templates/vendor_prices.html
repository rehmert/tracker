Vendor price comparison

Add offers manually or import price sheets, then compare cost per vial. Prices are informational; matching product names do not establish equivalent quality or safety.

Compare offers

Prices are normalized by vial count; offers initially appear grouped by compound and content; click a column heading to sort all offers. The lowest known delivered total is highlighted for offers with the same compound, content, and vial quantity (ties are highlighted). Offers without a shipping policy are excluded from that comparison. Shipping estimates assume a separate order containing only the listed package. Combined orders may qualify for free shipping and should not be charged shipping per line item.

Add vendor offer manually

Enter the contents of one vial and the price of the selected package. A half-kit is 5 vials; a kit is 10 vials. Package prices are converted to a comparable price per vial.

Import vendor items CSV

Upload a CSV with columns vendor,item_name,content_amount,content_unit,package_type,package_price,product_url. The last two optional columns are content_unit (defaults to mg) and product_url. Package types: vial, half-kit, kit. Each row can name a different vendor. Existing offers are not overwritten.
